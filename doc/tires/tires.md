# Tire Options #
Tires are relatively expensive compared to the cost of a basic car
setup. If you are planning on making several cars for a class or 
program, you may want to consider [making your own tires](diytires.md).

If you are only making a small number of cars, it is likely more time
and cost effective to buy a set of tires.

## Tire Sizes ##
The default files in this project have a 20mm diamter. The front wheels
are narrow, at 8.5mm. The rear wheels are wider, at 11mm.

There are several different sizes of tires available, and you can modify
the `08_Wheels.FCStd` file to fit your needs.

## Tire Hardness ##
Hardness is measured on the [Shore scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shore_durometer).
A very soft tire is around 10-Shore, and a very hard tire is around 50-Shore,
in typical RC usage. Because these printed 1:28 are small and light, we will
need to stay toward the lower end of that range to end up with a performant 
car setup. 

## Traction Disks ##
On very fast cars, you will often see disks attached to the outside of the 
front wheels. These disks are usually just slightly less in diameter than the
actual tire (maybe 21mm) and actually reduce front wheel traction in hard corners
to allow the car to understeer (go more straight) in a corner, rather than 
flipping over (a traction roll), or spinning the rear of the car around (oversteer).

The basic wheel files in this project don't account for traction disks, but
you can easily add them to the model files once you start to hone in a desired
car and tire setup.

## Simple Tire Options ##
### Kyosho Mini-Z Tires ###
The "stock" rubber slick tires that come with most Kyosho Mini-Z's are stiff
and slippery, but durable. These are not a great choice unless durability is
your desired factor.

Look for the "Racing Radial" tires from Kyosho for a higher performance tire,
in 20 or 30 Shore (hardness).
- [8.5mm Radial Racing Tire](https://www.amainhobbies.com/kyosho-miniz-8.5mm-racing-radial-tire-4-30-shore-kyomzw37-30/p385402?v=212141)
- [11mm Radial Racing Tire](https://www.amainhobbies.com/kyosho-miniz-11mm-wide-racing-radial-tire-4-20-shore-kyomzw38-20b/p385403?v=1541305)

### PN Racing Tires ###
These are considered "high end" tires, made of silicone and urethane. They
usually run about twice as much per tire as the Kyosho or other budget options
- [11mm KS-M Low Profile Super Soft](https://www.kenonhobby.com/PN-Racing-Mini-Z-KS-M-Compound-RCP-Low-Profile-Slick-11mm-Tire-Super-Soft-30-Shore-2pc_p_49472.html)
- [8.5mm KS Low Profile Super Soft](https://www.kenonhobby.com/PN-Racing-Mini-Z-KS-Compound-RCP-Low-Profile-Slick-85mm-Tire-SUPER-SOFT-30-Shore-2pc_p_49466.html)
